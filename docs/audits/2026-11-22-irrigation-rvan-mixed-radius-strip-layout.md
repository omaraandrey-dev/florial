# Irrigation assistant — R-VAN mixed-model, adjustable-radius and strip-layout redesign

Date: 2026-11-22  
Status: implementation in progress; no Production release

## Owner-approved direction

- Use Rain Bird R-VAN as the preferred nozzle family. Do not substitute HE-VAN when an R-VAN layout has not yet been proven; return an explicit unsupported / engineer-review result instead.
- Select model and adjustable radius per sprinkler position. A single lawn may combine R-VAN14, R-VAN18 and R-VAN24 where the geometry requires it.
- Choose the radius from the model's manufacturer range and the actual head-to-head geometry. Radius adjustment is performed while water is running.
- Select the arc/sector per position (corner, edge, reflex corner or full-circle interior) from supported manufacturer data.
- Place mandatory corner sprinklers first, distribute additional heads along the perimeter, then add internal rows only when the perimeter layout does not meet coverage and head-to-head checks.
- Add strip patterns for narrow lawn strips: R-VAN-LCS (left-corner strip), R-VAN-RCS (right-corner strip), and R-VAN-SST (side strip). Do not treat a strip nozzle as a circular sector.
- Keep the geometry intake approximate: remove the checkbox asking the customer to confirm that dimensions were measured. A valid contour generated from entered dimensions is automatically accepted as the customer's approximate input; the result must still say that the calculation is preliminary.

## Manufacturer evidence

Primary source: Rain Bird R-VAN Technical Specifications, document D41159B, 2024:  
https://www.rainbird.com/sites/default/files/media/documents/2025-10/026549_rb_rvantechspecsheet_metric_f.pdf

- R-VAN14 range: 2.4–4.6 m.
- R-VAN18 range: 4.0–5.5 m.
- R-VAN24 range: 5.2–7.3 m.
- Standard adjustable-arc products support 45°–270°; full-circle variants are separate SKUs.
- Recommended operating pressure: 3.1 bar; manufacturer operating range: 2.1–3.8 bar.
- Manufacturer says arc and radius are adjusted while water is running.
- Strip products: LCS 1.5 × 4.6 m; RCS 1.5 × 4.6 m; SST 1.5 × 9.1 m at the nominal 3.1 bar point. Strip size is adjustable, but the exact adjustment limits must not be invented.
- Manufacturer performance chart identifies strip spacing as straight-line spacing based on 50% overlap. This is not the circular head-to-head rule.
- Manufacturer warns not to reduce R-VAN14 below 2.4 m, R-VAN18 below 4.0 m, or R-VAN24 below 5.2 m.

Performance data already present in `src/lib/irrigation/rainBirdRvanPerformanceData.mjs` includes all nine product/SKU bindings and exact points at 2.1, 2.4, 2.8, 3.1, 3.4 and 3.8 bar. Its circular arc coverage is currently only a subset of the manufacturer's complete chart. The engine must not assume that the current subset is the complete arc catalogue.

## Important engineering constraints

- A geometric radius may be adjusted within a manufacturer's allowed model range, but the engine must distinguish the selected geometric radius from the exact tabulated nominal performance point. Do not invent flow or precipitation at an arbitrary adjustment setting. If a hydraulic result depends on an unverified adjusted operating point, mark it not evaluated / engineer review.
- LCS, RCS and SST require their own footprint geometry, orientation and overlap checks. They must not pass through the circular-sector coverage function.
- Strip size adjustment is documented by the manufacturer, but exact adjustment endpoints are not stated in the retrieved technical specification. For widths outside exact published points, do not claim an exact manufacturer-verified fit.
- Sampled coverage is not a continuous proof. Overspray, nozzle/body compatibility, hydraulics, pipe losses, zoning and precipitation uniformity remain separate gates.
- For L-shaped lawns, all convex and reflex corners must be considered. Strip candidates must not be generated by bounding-box assumptions that cover cut-out areas.
- No Sanity, D1, Cloudflare settings/secrets, Resend, Production data or deployment changes are in scope.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. 10 × 5 m: R-VAN18 corner/perimeter layout, no unnecessary centre-width row if perimeter coverage and head-to-head are satisfied.
2. 20 × 10 m: R-VAN24 corners and a longitudinal internal row if required; internal heads may use the same R-VAN24 with adjusted radius only within its permitted minimum, or another R-VAN model if the geometric and precipitation checks justify it.
3. L-shape: mandatory heads at every convex/reflex corner, perimeter heads, and internal line(s) only where needed; never cover the cut-out.
4. Narrow strips around 1–2 m: evaluate LCS/RCS/SST according to orientation and exact published size/pressure evidence; no circular HE-VAN fallback.
5. Mixed site: allow R-VAN14/18/24 at different positions when each selected model/radius/arc passes its own constraints.
6. Every output lists model, selected radius or strip dimensions, arc/pattern, direction, pressure basis, exact source point, and unresolved checks.
