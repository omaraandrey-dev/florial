## 5. SEO risk

The two domains appear to target many of the same commercial and local search themes:
- “продажба на палми в Несебър”
- “студоустойчиви палми за България”
- palm species names and botanical names
- olive tree sales and planting
- Nessebar / Bulgaria delivery and landscaping services

This can split relevance, backlinks, clicks and brand signals between two domains. It can also create uncertainty about which site Google should show for the same business and local intent. It is a **potential cannibalization / duplicate-entity risk**, not a proven penalty or confirmed ranking loss.

The main domain has the stronger role as the actual multi-language e-commerce platform, with published Sanity product records and product URLs. The Google Business Profile also directs users to the main domain.

## 6. Recommendation — no changes applied

### Primary recommendation

1. Keep `https://florial-bg.com/` as the primary website and the Google Business Profile destination.
2. Do not publish a second copy of the palm catalogue or repeat the same product SEO landing pages on the secondary domain.
3. Before deciding the secondary domain's fate, establish who controls its registrar, DNS and hosting/CMS account.
4. Obtain read-only access or an owner-supplied export so that redirects, canonicals, robots, sitemap, analytics and page inventory can be checked.
5. Compare the secondary domain's Search Console data with the main property before any redirect decision, if a separate property can be accessed.

### Two possible future strategies

**Option A — retire the secondary domain and consolidate.**  
If it has no independent business purpose and its search value is negligible, plan one-to-one 301 redirects to the most relevant Florial pages (not every URL blindly to the homepage), retain the domain registration, and monitor Search Console after release. This requires domain/hosting control and explicit owner approval.

**Option B — retain it as a genuinely separate campaign/lead-generation site.**  
Only do this if the owner wants it to serve a distinct purpose. The content must be differentiated, product/species copy must not be duplicated, the calls-to-action should lead into the primary store where appropriate, and the business identity / website links must be consistent. A separate site should not imitate the full primary catalogue.

Do not add cross-domain canonical tags mechanically. A canonical is appropriate only when pages are duplicate or substantially equivalent; a redirect strategy needs a URL-by-URL mapping.

## 7. Content accuracy caution

The second site's indexed homepage makes specific temperature-resistance claims (for example, Trachycarpus around -17°C, Chamaerops around -13°C and olive trees around -15°C), and includes botanical spelling variants such as “Phoenix rubelenie”. These claims and names were not validated in this SEO review. Do not copy them into Florial Sanity content without a separate horticultural check and clearly stated growing-condition context.
